Privacy Policy

AutoTube is a private tool used by its owner to publish the owner's own short videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts.

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information from TikTok. Tokens are stored only on the owner's private server and are used solely to publish the owner's videos.

The app does not collect data from other users, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings.
